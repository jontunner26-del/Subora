About Subora

Subora is a Nigerian marketplace where approved vendors sell shared subscriptions, online courses, software seats and other digital goods. Every payment is held in escrow until the buyer confirms that it works.

What is Subora?

Many digital tools are priced for one person but can be shared safely between a few. Subora lets approved vendors sell those spare places, along with courses, templates and other digital goods, at a fraction of the usual price.

You pay from a wallet you fund yourself. Your money is held by Subora until you confirm that what you bought works. Only then is the vendor paid. It is the safety of a shop, with the prices of a shared plan. Anyone can browse the marketplace, and you only need an account to buy.

Who is Subora for?

Subora is for buyers in Nigeria who want the lower price of a shared plan without the risk of paying a stranger, and for vendors who want a trusted place to sell spare places and digital products. Everything is priced in naira, and you can fund your wallet by card, bank transfer or USSD.

What does Subora stand for?

  • Escrow on every order. Your payment is held until you confirm that your access works.
  • Vendors approved by hand. Vendors give identity documents and bank details, and every listing is reviewed before it goes live.
  • Permanent records. Chats, wallet entries and dispute statements cannot be edited or deleted, so any problem can be traced and settled fairly.
  • Plain fees. Buying is free. Vendors pay a 10% fee on each sale and a flat ₦100 per withdrawal.

How does Subora make money?

Subora charges vendors 10% of each sale and a flat ₦100 for each withdrawal to a bank, plus a monthly marketing levy that pays for adverts that bring buyers to the marketplace. Buyers pay only the price shown on the listing. The full wording is in the Terms and Conditions.
