Buying safely on Subora

Subora holds your payment in escrow, checks every vendor by hand, and keeps a permanent record of every chat, payment and dispute, so problems can be settled fairly.

EscrowYour money is held until you confirm your access works

PermanentChats and payments cannot be edited or deleted

How does escrow protect my money?

When you pay, Subora holds the money instead of sending it to the vendor. The vendor is paid once you confirm that your login, code or link works, or after 48 hours if you have not reported a problem. While you are checking your access, the vendor never holds your money.

What if my access stops working?

Tap Report a problem on your subscription card. The vendor has 48 hours to respond, and your payment stays frozen in escrow while they do. A refund can be full, for the days you did not use, or a custom amount, and it goes back to your Subora wallet straight away. If you and the vendor disagree, Subora decides using the full chat and payment record, writes down the reason, and decides within 48 hours of both sides sharing their evidence. Read the full refund policy.

How are vendors checked?

Vendors apply and are approved by hand. They give identity documents, a selfie and bank details before they can sell, and every listing is reviewed before it goes live. Payouts only go to a bank account in the vendor’s own verified name.

A blue tick marks a verified vendor who passed these checks. A gold tick marks a listing sold directly by Subora.

How are gift card, crypto, account and number sellers checked?

These sellers carry more risk, so they have extra steps: identity verification, a video selfie, a proof of address and a ₦100,000 security deposit. Confirmed claims give a seller strikes. Three confirmed claims pause their listings, five suspend the account, and fraud closes it and uses the deposit to repay buyers. Claim windows are short and clear:

  • Gift cards: report within 30 minutes of revealing the code, with a screenshot. The vendor has 2 hours to replace it or refund you, then Subora decides.
  • Crypto: refunded in full if nothing arrives within 1 hour of payment, or if the wrong amount is sent.
  • OTP numbers: refunded automatically if no SMS arrives within 20 minutes.
  • Accounts: can be claimed within 24 hours of handover.

How do I avoid scams on Subora?

  • Pay only through Subora. Payments outside Subora have no escrow, no refunds and no dispute help.
  • Report any chat message that asks you to pay or talk outside Subora. Those messages are hidden and reviewed.
  • Subora staff never ask for your password, your codes or payment outside the app.
  • Turn on two-step verification in your profile, so a new device, a withdrawal or a bank change needs a code sent to your phone.

What are the third-party rules about shared accounts?

Subora is a marketplace: we provide the place where buyers and vendors meet, and we do not own, supply or control what vendors list. Some services, such as streaming, AI and software providers, have their own rules about sharing accounts or reselling access, and some do not allow it. Each vendor is responsible for checking those rules before listing. A provider can also change or end access at any time, and the refund rules then apply. See section 9 of the Terms and Conditions.

I am a brand or rights holder. How do I report a listing?

Send us a rights holder notice. We review notices within 24 hours and pause the listing while we check. See how to contact us.
