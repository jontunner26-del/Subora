Subora marketplace: buy and share digital access, safely

Subora is a marketplace for shared subscriptions, online courses, software seats and digital goods. This page explains how it works. Browse the marketplace without an account, and create a free account when you are ready to buy.

What is Subora?

Many digital tools are priced for one person but can be shared safely between a few. Subora lets approved vendors sell those spare places, along with courses, templates and other digital goods, at a fraction of the usual price.

You pay from a wallet you fund yourself. Your money is held by Subora until you confirm that what you bought works. Only then is the vendor paid. It is the safety of a shop, with the prices of a shared plan. Anyone can browse the marketplace, and you only need an account to buy.

What can I buy on Subora?

Listings fall into a few simple groups. Prices are shown in Nigerian naira (₦).

  • AI assistants. A private place on plans for chat, writing and image tools.
  • Streaming and music. One screen or one seat on a family or premium plan.
  • Design and software. Team seats for tools used at work and school.
  • Courses and templates. Recorded lessons, project files, templates and content packs.
  • Accounts and numbers. Aged social accounts and virtual numbers, with extra checks on every seller.

How does buying on Subora work?

  1. Browse, then create a free account. Anyone can look through listings and compare prices. When you are ready to buy, sign up with your email and phone number and confirm with a one-time code.
  2. Fund your wallet. Add money by card, bank transfer or USSD. It is credited when the payment is confirmed.
  3. Pick a listing and read the vendor’s terms. Each vendor states what you get and the rules of use. You accept them before you pay.
  4. Pay into escrow. Your money is held, not sent. Your access appears on your subscription card.
  5. Confirm it works. The vendor is paid once you confirm, or after 48 hours with no problem reported.

How does Subora keep my money safe?

Safety is built into every order, not added afterwards.

  • Escrow. Vendors never hold your money while you are checking access.
  • Refunds. If access breaks, report it from the card. A refund can be full or for the days you did not use.
  • Disputes with a record. If you and a vendor disagree, Subora decides using the full chat and payment record, and writes down the reason.
  • Permanent history. Chats, payments and disputes cannot be edited or deleted by anyone, so every problem can be traced.

How do I get my login or code?

Many listings deliver automatically. A sign-in code or an access link arrives on your card within seconds, with no need to wait for the vendor. If you need a person, you can chat with the vendor before and after you buy. If they are away, you can send an urgent nudge by SMS and email, and Subora support steps in if there is still no reply.

Who sells on Subora?

Vendors are few and approved by hand. They give their identity documents and bank details before they can sell, and every listing is reviewed before it goes live. Sellers are easy to tell apart by their tick: a blue tick marks a verified vendor who passed our identity checks, and a gold tick marks a listing sold directly by Subora.

What does Subora cost?

Creating an account and buying are free. You pay only the price on the listing, from your wallet. Vendors pay a 10% fee on each sale and a small flat fee on each withdrawal, which keeps the marketplace running and funds the checks above.
