Refund policy

If something you bought does not work, you can report it and get a full or prorated refund. This page shows the rules for each kind of listing.

Last updated 19 October 2026

How do I get a refund?

Tap Report a problem on the subscription card. The vendor has 48 hours to respond. If you disagree, Subora decides using the full record. Every refund is recorded in both wallets. Read more about refunds and disputes.

Subscriptions and seats

Report a problem from the card. The vendor has 48 hours to fix it or refund. Refunds can be full, or prorated for the days left.

Courses and downloads

Refundable if the files don’t open or don’t match the description, reported within 48 hours.

Gift cards

Report within 30 minutes of revealing the code, with a screenshot. The vendor has 2 hours to replace it or refund, then Subora decides.

Crypto

Refunded in full if nothing arrives within 1 hour of payment, or if the vendor sends the wrong amount. Not refundable if you entered a wrong address.

Accounts and OTP numbers

Accounts can be claimed within 24 hours of handover. OTP numbers refund automatically if no SMS arrives in 20 minutes.

Where refunds go

Back to your Subora wallet straight away. You can spend it or ask support to send it to your bank.
