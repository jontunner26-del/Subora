Contact Subora

Chat with Subora support every day from 8am to 10pm West Africa Time. Chat replies usually come within 15 to 30 minutes in support hours, and email replies within 24 hours.

How can I contact Subora?

Live chat
In the app, open the Help Centre and tap Chat with support
Hours
8am to 10pm West Africa Time, every day
Typical reply
Within 15 to 30 minutes in chat hours
Disputes
Decided within 48 hours of both sides sharing evidence
Email
support@subora.com.ng

What should I do if an order goes wrong?

Tap Report a problem on the subscription card for that order. The vendor has 48 hours to respond and your payment stays in escrow meanwhile. If the vendor does not reply, tap Nudge vendor in the chat: they are alerted by SMS and email, and Subora support steps in if there is still no answer. See how refunds and disputes work.

I am a brand or rights holder. How do I report a listing?

A service provider, brand or rights holder can ask us to remove a listing that breaks their rules or rights. We review notices within 24 hours and pause the listing while we check. Email support@subora.com.ng with the brand name, the listing and what rule or right it breaks.

Subora staff never ask for your password, your codes or payment outside the app.
