Buying

How do I buy?

Fund your wallet, open a listing, pick the plan length, tick that you have read the terms, and pay. Your access appears on your subscription card or order.

What is escrow?

Your money is held by Subora until you confirm that access works, or 48 hours pass with no problem reported. Only then is the vendor paid.

Can I buy 3 or 6 months at once?

Yes, on most subscriptions. Longer plans cost less per month.

Wallet and payments

How do I fund my wallet?

Tap Fund wallet and pay by card, bank transfer or USSD. Your wallet is credited when the payment is confirmed.

Why can’t I pay a vendor directly?

Payments outside Subora have no escrow, no refunds and no dispute help. Chat messages that ask for outside payment are hidden and reviewed.

How do coupons work?

Enter the code in your cart. Vendor codes only work on that vendor’s listings.

Codes and access

How do I get a sign-in code?

Sign in with the email on your subscription card, then tap Get sign-in code. The code arrives in seconds and works once.

The code didn’t come

Wait two minutes and try again. If nothing arrives, the vendor is alerted automatically. You can also message them from the card.

Gift cards, crypto and accounts

A gift card says it was already used

Report it from the order within 30 minutes of revealing it, with a screenshot of the error. The vendor has 2 hours to send a new card or refund you, then Subora decides using the reveal log.

How is crypto delivered?

You enter your own wallet address before paying. The vendor sends on-chain and adds the transaction ID. Payment is released only after the network confirms it.

My OTP number got no SMS

If no SMS arrives within 20 minutes, your money goes back to your wallet automatically.

How are these sellers checked?

They pass identity verification, a video selfie and a proof of address, and pay a security deposit. Confirmed claims give them strikes. 3 strikes pause them, 5 suspend them.

Support and contact

When is support available?

Chat support answers every day from 8am to 10pm (West Africa Time). Outside those hours, leave a message and we reply first thing.

How fast will I get a reply?

Chat: usually within 15 minutes in support hours. Email: within 24 hours. Disputes are decided within 48 hours of both sides sharing their evidence.

The vendor is not replying

Tap Nudge vendor in the chat. The vendor gets an SMS and email. If there is no reply in the time they set (1 to 8 hours), Subora support steps in.

I am a brand or rights holder

Use the Rights holder notice link in the footer. The listing is paused while we check.

Refunds and disputes

How do I get a refund?

Tap Report a problem on the subscription card. The vendor has 48 hours to respond. If you disagree, Subora decides using the full record.

Are refunds prorated?

Vendors can refund in full, for the days left, or a custom amount. Every refund is recorded in both wallets.

Account and security

How do I turn on two-step verification?

Go to My profile, then Security. With it on, logging in on a new device, withdrawing and changing bank details need a code sent to your phone.

I think someone else logged in

Open My profile, log out all other devices and change your password. Then message support.

Selling on Subora

Who can sell?

Vendors apply and are approved by hand. Verified vendors unlock Pro tools. Gift card, crypto and account sellers need asset seller status.

What does it cost?

10% of each sale, a flat ₦100 per withdrawal and a monthly marketing levy. See the Terms.

Still have a question?

Chat with support every day from 8am to 10pm West Africa Time.
