Terms and Conditions

These terms explain how buyers and vendors use Subora, how escrow and fees work, and what each side is responsible for.

Last updated 19 October 2026

1. Your account

You must give accurate details and keep your login private. You are responsible for all activity on your account. Vendors must complete identity verification to use Pro features.

2. Listings and resale rights

List only access, courses or files that you are allowed to resell. Stolen, illegal or unauthorised items are banned. Subora may reject, pause or remove any listing.

3. Payments, escrow and fees

Buyer payments are held in escrow until the buyer confirms access, or 48 hours pass with no dispute. Subora charges vendors 10% of every sale. On a ₦20,000 sale the vendor receives ₦18,000, and on a ₦10,000 sale the vendor receives ₦9,000. Each withdrawal to a bank costs a flat ₦100, with a ₦2,000 minimum.

4. Refunds and disputes

A refund request opens a case and freezes the escrow. The vendor has 48 hours to approve it or explain a decline. If they decline or do not reply, Subora support decides using the full record and writes down the reason.

5. Permanent records

Chats, wallet entries and dispute statements cannot be edited or deleted by buyers, vendors or staff. They are kept for as long as the law and dispute handling require.

6. Vendor marketing levy

Every vendor agrees that Subora will deduct a monthly marketing levy of ₦2,000 from the vendor’s available wallet balance on the 1st of each month. The levy pays for adverts that bring buyers to the marketplace. If the balance is too low on the due date, the amount is collected from the next earnings, and listings may be paused if it stays unpaid for 14 days. Subora will give 30 days’ notice before changing the amount.

7. Prohibited conduct

No fraud, harassment, taking payments outside Subora to avoid fees, or attempts to tamper with records.

8. Suspension and changes

Subora may suspend accounts that break these terms. We will tell you when the terms change.

9. Third-party rules and your responsibility

Subora is a marketplace. We provide the place where buyers and vendors meet, and we do not own, supply or control what vendors list. Many services, such as streaming, AI and software providers, have their own rules about sharing accounts, reselling access or using one plan across several people. Some of them do not allow it. Each vendor is solely responsible for checking those rules before listing, for having the right to sell what they list, and for any claim, loss, suspension, fine or legal action that comes from a listing. If a third party makes a claim about a listing, the vendor who listed it answers to that claim and agrees to cover Subora for any cost it faces because of it (an indemnity). Buyers should also know that a provider may change or end access at any time. When that happens, the refund rules in section 4 apply. Subora removes listings that it is told break a provider’s rules, and it may share vendor details with a rightful claimant or an authority when the law asks it to. Nothing here limits any right you have under the law.
