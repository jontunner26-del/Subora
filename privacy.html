Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data Subora collects, why we use it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it.

Last updated 19 October 2026

What we collect

Your name, email, phone number, orders, wallet activity, chats and the device you use. Vendors also give identity documents, a selfie and bank details for verification.

Why we use it

To run your account and orders, hold payments in escrow, deliver codes, settle disputes, stop fraud and meet legal duties.

Who we share it with

Payment processors (such as Paystack or Flutterwave) to move money, an identity-check provider for vendor verification, and messaging providers for WhatsApp, email and SMS alerts. Vendors see only what they need to deliver your order. We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

Chats, wallet entries and dispute records are kept as permanent records for as long as the law and dispute handling require. Other data is deleted or anonymised when it is no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or export your data, and to delete what we are not required to keep, under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Cookies

Essential cookies keep you logged in and your cart safe. Optional analytics cookies are used only if you accept them.

Contact

Questions about your data: use Chat with support in the app.
